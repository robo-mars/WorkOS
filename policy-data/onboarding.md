# Your first week
Policy ID: onboarding · Owner: People Operations · Reviewed: 2026-10-01
Before your first day, complete identity verification through the approved HR portal and sign your employment documents. On Monday, arrive at reception at 09:00 with photo identification. Attend the 10:00 orientation, collect your laptop from IT, and enroll in multi-factor authentication. Your manager confirms team onboarding and your first-week schedule. Request a monitor through an IT hardware ticket. Never send identity documents or passwords through the workplace assistant.
