# Desks and meeting rooms
Policy ID: booking · Owner: Workplace Operations · Reviewed: 2026-10-01
Desks may be reserved for a full working day, 09:00–17:00 local office time. Meeting rooms should be booked only for the time needed. Dubai and London offices offer bookable resources. Engineering has a dedicated desk zone. Cancel unused reservations. Cancellation requires your confirmation. A reservation never grants building access.
